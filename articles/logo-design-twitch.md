# Logo Design for Twitch: Level Up Your Stream

Your Twitch logo is your streamer identity. It needs to be memorable and represent your gaming persona. Here’s how to approach **logo design for Twitch**.

## 1. Keep It Simple
A simple logo is easier to recognize in a small profile picture.

## 2. Use a Gaming Mascot or Symbol
A strong mascot or symbol can represent your stream.

## 3. Ensure It Fits in a Circle
Twitch uses circular profile pictures.

## 4. Use Bright, Energetic Colors
Bright colors stand out on the platform.

## 5. Create Overlays and Panels
Your logo should work with your stream overlays and panels.
