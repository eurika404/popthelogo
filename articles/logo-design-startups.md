# Logo Design for Startups: Launch with a Strong Brand

Your logo is the foundation of your brand. Here’s how to approach **logo design for startups**.

## Key Elements of Startup Logos

*   **Simplicity:** Easy to remember and scale.
*   **Relevance:** Reflects your industry and values.
*   **Versatility:** Works across all platforms and media.

## Logo Ideas for Startups

*   **Abstract Symbol:** A unique shape that becomes synonymous with your brand.
*   **Wordmark:** A clean, typographic logo.
*   **Lettermark:** Use your startup’s initials in a stylized way.
