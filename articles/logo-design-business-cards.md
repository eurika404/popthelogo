# Logo Design for Business Cards: Make a Lasting Impression

Your business card is a tangible representation of your brand. Your logo needs to look crisp and professional on a small piece of paper. Here’s how to approach **logo design for business cards**.

## 1. Use Vector Files
Vector files (AI, EPS, SVG) ensure your logo looks sharp at any print size.

## 2. Keep It Simple
Avoid overly complex designs that can look cluttered on a small card.

## 3. Consider the Layout
Your logo should be the focal point of your business card.

## 4. Choose the Right Colors
Ensure your colors look good in print (CMYK) and on screen (RGB).

## 5. Leave White Space
Don’t overcrowd your card. White space helps your logo breathe.
