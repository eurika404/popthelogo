# Logo Design for Workshops: Creative and Hands-On

Your workshop logo needs to be creative and hands-on. It should represent the skills being taught. Here’s how to approach **logo design for workshops**.

## 1. Capture the Skill
Your logo should hint at what participants will learn.

## 2. Use Creative Typography
Fonts that feel artistic and inspiring.

## 3. Use Warm Colors
Colors that create a welcoming and creative atmosphere.

## 4. Keep It Simple
A simple logo is easier to remember.

## 5. Create Variations
Have a full logo for your marketing materials and a simplified icon for social media.
