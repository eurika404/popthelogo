# Logo Design for Websites: Best Practices

Your website is often the first place customers interact with your brand. Your logo needs to look great and load fast. Here’s how to approach **logo design for websites**.

## 1. Use SVG Files
SVGs are scalable, lightweight, and look sharp on all screens.

## 2. Optimize for Speed
Compress your logo files to ensure fast loading times.

## 3. Ensure Responsiveness
Your logo should look good on desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.

## 4. Place It Strategically
Typically, logos are placed in the top-left corner of a website.

## 5. Make It Clickable
Your logo should link back to your homepage.
