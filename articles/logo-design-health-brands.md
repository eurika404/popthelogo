# Logo Design for Health Brands: Trust & Wellness

Your logo needs to convey trust, health, and professionalism. Here’s how to approach **logo design for health brands**.

## Key Elements of Health Logos

*   **Imagery:** Leaves, hearts, crosses, human figures, and abstract wellness symbols.
*   **Typography:** Clean, accessible fonts that feel safe and reliable.
*   **Colors:** Green (health/nature), Blue (trust/calm), White (purity), Pink (care).

## Logo Ideas for Health Brands

*   **Symbol + Wordmark:** A wellness icon with your brand name.
*   **Lettermark:** Use your initials in a clean, professional way.
*   **Abstract:** A shape that represents balance and well-being.
