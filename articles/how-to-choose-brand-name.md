# How to Choose a Brand Name: A Comprehensive Guide

Your brand name is the first thing customers hear. It needs to be memorable, easy to pronounce, and reflective of your brand’s personality. Here’s how to choose a brand name.

## 1. Define Your Brand Personality
Are you playful, professional, or innovative? Your name should match your vibe.

## 2. Brainstorm Keywords
List words related to your industry, values, and target audience.

## 3. Keep It Simple
Short, easy-to-spell names are more memorable.

## 4. Check Availability
Make sure the domain name and social media handles are available.

## 5. Test It Out
Say it out loud. Ask for feedback from friends and potential customers.
