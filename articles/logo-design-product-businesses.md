# Logo Design for Product Businesses: Memorable and Marketable

Your product business logo needs to be memorable and marketable. It should make your products stand out. Here’s how to approach **logo design for product businesses**.

## 1. Represent Your Products
Your logo should hint at what you sell.

## 2. Use Marketable Typography
Fonts that feel appealing to your target audience.

## 3. Use Eye-Catching Colors
Colors that make your products pop.

## 4. Keep It Simple
A simple logo is easier to remember.

## 5. Create Variations
Have a full logo for your packaging and a simplified icon for social media.
