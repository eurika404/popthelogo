# Logo Design for Schools: Academic and Trustworthy

Your school logo needs to be academic and trustworthy. It should represent your institution’s values. Here’s how to approach **logo design for schools**.

## 1. Use Academic Imagery
Books, graduation caps, and shields work well.

## 2. Use Traditional Typography
Serif fonts can convey tradition and authority.

## 3. Use School Colors
Use your school’s official colors.

## 4. Keep It Timeless
Avoid trendy designs that will look dated in a few years.

## 5. Create Variations
Have a full logo for your letterhead and a simplified icon for social media.
