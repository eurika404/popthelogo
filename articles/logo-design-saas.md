# Logo Design for SaaS Products: Scalable and Trustworthy

Your SaaS product logo needs to be scalable and trustworthy. It will appear on your website, app, and marketing materials. Here’s how to approach **logo design for SaaS products**.

## 1. Use SVG Files
SVGs are scalable, lightweight, and look sharp on all screens.

## 2. Keep It Simple
A simple logo is easier to recognize in a small icon.

## 3. Use a Recognizable Symbol
A strong symbol can represent your SaaS product.

## 4. Ensure It Works on Different Backgrounds
Your logo should look good on both light and dark backgrounds.

## 5. Create Variations
Have a full logo for your website and a simplified icon for your app.
