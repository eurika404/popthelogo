# Logo Design for Uniforms: Professional and Cohesive

Your employee uniforms represent your brand. Your logo needs to be professional and cohesive. Here’s how to approach **logo design for uniforms**.

## 1. Design for Embroidery
Simple designs with clean lines work best for embroidery.

## 2. Consider the Fabric
Your logo might be printed or embroidered on different fabrics.

## 3. Use a Limited Color Palette
Embroidery works best with a limited number of thread colors.

## 4. Keep It Small
Your logo should be subtle, not overwhelming.

## 5. Test on Mockups
See how your logo looks on different uniform styles.
