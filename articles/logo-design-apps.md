# Logo Design for Apps: Iconic and Recognizable

Your app icon is the face of your mobile application. It needs to be iconic and recognizable on a crowded home screen. Here’s how to approach **logo design for apps**.

## 1. Keep It Simple
A simple, bold design is easier to recognize in a small icon.

## 2. Use a Recognizable Symbol
A strong symbol can communicate your app’s purpose at a glance.

## 3. Avoid Text
Text is often illegible in small app icons.

## 4. Use Bright, Contrasting Colors
Bright colors stand out on a phone screen.

## 5. Test on Different Backgrounds
Your icon should look good on both light and dark wallpapers.
