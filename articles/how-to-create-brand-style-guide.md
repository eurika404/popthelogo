# How to Create a Brand Style Guide: A Step-by-Step Guide

A brand style guide is a document that outlines how your brand should be presented to the world. It ensures consistency across all touchpoints. Here’s how to create one.

## 1. Define Your Brand Core
Include your mission, vision, and values.

## 2. Outline Your Logo Usage
Specify how your logo should be used, including minimum sizes, spacing, and color variations.

## 3. Document Your Color Palette
Include HEX, RGB, and CMYK codes for your primary and secondary colors.

## 4. Specify Your Typography
List the fonts you use for headings, body text, and other elements.

## 5. Define Your Brand Voice
Describe how your brand speaks. Is it formal, friendly, or playful?

## 6. Provide Imagery Guidelines
Specify the style of photos, illustrations, and icons you use.
