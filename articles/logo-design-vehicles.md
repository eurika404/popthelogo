# Logo Design for Vehicles: Mobile Billboards

Your company vehicles are mobile billboards. Your logo needs to be eye-catching and readable at highway speeds. Here’s how to approach **logo design for vehicles**.

## 1. Design for Motion
Your logo needs to be recognizable even when the vehicle is moving.

## 2. Use Bold Typography
Thick, clear fonts are easier to read from a distance.

## 3. Use High-Contrast Colors
Bright, contrasting colors stand out on any vehicle color.

## 4. Keep It Simple
Avoid complex details that can be lost on a moving vehicle.

## 5. Consider the Vehicle’s Shape
Your logo should complement the vehicle’s contours.
