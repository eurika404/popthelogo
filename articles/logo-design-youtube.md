# Logo Design for YouTube: Stand Out on the Platform

Your YouTube logo is your channel’s identity. It needs to be memorable and professional. Here’s how to approach **logo design for YouTube**.

## 1. Keep It Simple
A simple logo is easier to recognize in a small profile picture.

## 2. Use a Recognizable Icon
A strong icon works well as a channel avatar.

## 3. Ensure It Fits in a Circle
YouTube uses circular profile pictures.

## 4. Use High-Contrast Colors
Bright, contrasting colors stand out on the platform.

## 5. Create a Banner Logo
Your banner logo should be wider and include your channel name.
