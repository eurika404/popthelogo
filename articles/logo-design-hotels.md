# Logo Design for Hotels: Luxury and Comfort

Your hotel logo needs to convey luxury, comfort, and hospitality. Here’s how to approach **logo design for hotels**.

## 1. Reflect the Experience
Is your hotel a luxury resort, a boutique hotel, or a budget-friendly stay?

## 2. Use Elegant Typography
Script fonts or elegant serifs can convey luxury.

## 3. Use Calming Colors
Blue, green, and gold can evoke feelings of relaxation and luxury.

## 4. Keep It Timeless
Avoid trendy designs that will look dated in a few years.

## 5. Create Variations
Have a full logo for your signage and a simplified icon for your app.
