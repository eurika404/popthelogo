# Logo Design for Merchandise: Wear Your Brand

Your merchandise—t-shirts, mugs, hats, and tote bags—is a walking advertisement for your brand. Your logo needs to look great on all of them. Here’s how to approach **logo design for merchandise**.

## 1. Design for Different Surfaces
Your logo might be embroidered, screen-printed, or engraved.

## 2. Use Simple Shapes
Complex designs can be difficult to reproduce on merchandise.

## 3. Consider the Color of the Merchandise
Your logo needs to stand out on both light and dark backgrounds.

## 4. Use Vector Files
Vector files ensure your logo can be scaled to any size without losing quality.

## 5. Test on Mockups
See how your logo looks on different merchandise before you order.
