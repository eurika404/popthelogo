# Logo Design for LinkedIn: Professional and Polished

Your LinkedIn logo is your professional identity. It needs to be polished and credible. Here’s how to approach **logo design for LinkedIn**.

## 1. Keep It Simple
A simple logo is easier to recognize in a small profile picture.

## 2. Use a Professional Symbol
A strong symbol can represent your company or personal brand.

## 3. Ensure It Fits in a Circle
LinkedIn uses circular profile pictures.

## 4. Use Professional Colors
Choose colors that convey trust and credibility.

## 5. Create a Banner Logo
Your banner logo should be wider and include your company name.
