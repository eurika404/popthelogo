# Logo Design for Social Media: Stand Out in the Feed

Your logo needs to be eye-catching and recognizable in a crowded social media feed. Here’s how to approach **logo design for social media**.

## 1. Keep It Simple
Simple logos are easier to recognize in small sizes.

## 2. Use a Recognizable Icon
A strong icon works well as a profile picture.

## 3. Ensure It Fits in a Circle
Most social media platforms use circular profile pictures.

## 4. Use High-Contrast Colors
Bright, contrasting colors stand out in a busy feed.

## 5. Create Variations
Have a full logo for your cover photo and a simplified icon for your profile picture.
