# Logo Design for Stationery: Classic and Professional

Your business stationery—letterheads, envelopes, and notepads—represents your brand in formal communications. Your logo needs to be classic and professional. Here’s how to approach **logo design for stationery**.

## 1. Use Vector Files
Vector files ensure your logo looks crisp on printed stationery.

## 2. Keep It Simple
A simple logo looks elegant on letterheads.

## 3. Use a Limited Color Palette
One or two colors work best for stationery.

## 4. Consider the Paper Stock
Your logo should look good on different paper types.

## 5. Maintain Clear Space
Leave enough white space around your logo.
