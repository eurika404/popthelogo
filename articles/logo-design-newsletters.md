# Logo Design for Newsletters: Professional and Engaging

Your newsletter logo adds a professional touch to your email communications. Here’s how to approach **logo design for newsletters**.

## 1. Keep It Small
Your logo should be small enough to not overwhelm the header.

## 2. Use a Simple Design
A simple logo is easier to recognize in a small size.

## 3. Use a Transparent Background
A PNG with a transparent background blends seamlessly with your design.

## 4. Place It Strategically
Typically, logos are placed in the header of a newsletter.

## 5. Match Your Brand Colors
Ensure your logo colors match your brand palette.
