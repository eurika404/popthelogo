# Logo Design for Packaging: Make Your Product Stand Out

Your product packaging is a silent salesperson. Your logo needs to be the hero of the shelf. Here’s how to approach **logo design for packaging**.

## 1. Design for the Shelf
Your logo needs to be recognizable from a distance.

## 2. Consider the Material
Your logo might be printed on cardboard, glass, plastic, or fabric.

## 3. Use High-Contrast Colors
Bright, contrasting colors stand out on a crowded shelf.

## 4. Keep It Simple
Avoid overly complex designs that can look messy on small packaging.

## 5. Test at Different Sizes
Ensure your logo looks good on both small labels and large boxes.
