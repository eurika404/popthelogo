# Logo Design for Email Signatures: Professional Touch

Your email signature is a digital business card. Your logo adds a professional touch to every email you send. Here’s how to approach **logo design for email signatures**.

## 1. Keep It Small
Your logo should be small enough to not overwhelm the signature.

## 2. Use a Simple Design
A simple logo is easier to recognize in a small size.

## 3. Link to Your Website
Make your logo clickable, linking to your homepage.

## 4. Use a Transparent Background
A PNG with a transparent background blends seamlessly with your signature.

## 5. Test on Different Email Clients
Ensure your logo looks good on Gmail, Outlook, and Apple Mail.
