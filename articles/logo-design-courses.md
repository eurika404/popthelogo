# Logo Design for Courses: Educational and Trustworthy

Your course logo needs to be educational and trustworthy. It should represent the value of your content. Here’s how to approach **logo design for courses**.

## 1. Capture the Subject
Your logo should hint at what students will learn.

## 2. Use Professional Typography
Fonts that feel modern and authoritative.

## 3. Use Educational Colors
Blue, green, and other colors that convey learning.

## 4. Keep It Simple
A simple logo is easier to remember.

## 5. Create Variations
Have a full logo for your course platform and a simplified icon for social media.
