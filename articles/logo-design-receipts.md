# Logo Design for Receipts: Simple and Clear

Your receipts are a final touchpoint with customers. Your logo adds a professional touch. Here’s how to approach **logo design for receipts**.

## 1. Keep It Small
Your logo should be small enough to fit on a receipt.

## 2. Use a Simple Design
A simple logo is easier to recognize in a small size.

## 3. Use a Monochrome Version
Many receipt printers only print in black and white.

## 4. Place It Strategically
Typically, logos are placed at the top of a receipt.

## 5. Test on Receipt Printers
Ensure your logo looks good on thermal paper.
