# The Branding Process for Startups: A Step-by-Step Guide

Branding is a journey, not a destination. Here’s a step-by-step guide to the **branding process for startups**.

## Step 1: Define Your Brand Strategy
Before you design anything, define your mission, vision, values, and target audience.

## Step 2: Create Your Brand Identity
This includes your logo, color palette, typography, and imagery.

## Step 3: Develop Your Brand Voice
How does your brand speak? Is it formal, friendly, or playful?

## Step 4: Build Your Brand Collateral
This includes your website, business cards, social media profiles, and marketing materials.

## Step 5: Launch and Monitor
Launch your brand and monitor how customers perceive it. Be ready to iterate.
