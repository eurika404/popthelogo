# Logo Design for Non-Profits: Communicate Your Mission

Non-profit logos need to convey trust, compassion, and purpose. Here’s how to approach **logo design for non-profits**.

## Key Elements of Non-Profit Logos

*   **Imagery:** Hands, hearts, globes, trees, and other symbols of help and growth.
*   **Typography:** Clean, accessible fonts that feel welcoming.
*   **Colors:** Blue (trust), Green (growth), Red (compassion), Yellow (hope).

## Logo Ideas for Non-Profits

*   **Symbol + Wordmark:** A meaningful icon with your organization's name.
*   **Emblem:** A classic look that conveys authority and stability.
*   **Abstract:** A modern shape that represents your cause.
