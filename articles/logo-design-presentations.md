# Logo Design for Presentations: Consistent and Professional

Your presentations represent your brand. Your logo should be consistent and professional on every slide. Here’s how to approach **logo design for presentations**.

## 1. Place It Strategically
Typically, logos are placed in the corner of each slide.

## 2. Keep It Simple
A simple logo doesn’t distract from your content.

## 3. Use the Same Logo
Consistency is key. Use the same logo on every slide.

## 4. Use High-Contrast Colors
Ensure your logo stands out against the slide background.

## 5. Don’t Oversize It
Your logo should be subtle, not overwhelming.
