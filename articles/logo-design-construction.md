# Logo Design for Construction Business: Build a Strong Image

Your construction logo needs to be as solid as the structures you build. It should convey strength, reliability, and professionalism. Here’s how to approach **logo design for a construction business**.

## Key Elements of Construction Logos

*   **Imagery:** Hard hats, tools, buildings, cranes, and geometric shapes.
*   **Typography:** Bold, heavy fonts that look sturdy.
*   **Colors:** Yellow (safety), Black (strength), Orange (energy), Blue (trust).

## Logo Ideas for Construction

*   **Emblem:** A classic, professional look.
*   **Icon + Wordmark:** A strong symbol with your company name.
*   **Abstract:** A geometric shape that represents building and structure.
