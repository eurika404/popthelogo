## 3. Technical Deep Dive: Logos & Formats

### Vector vs Raster Logo Files

Understanding **what is a vector logo** is critical.
*   **SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics):** Infinitely scalable without losing quality. Best for web.
*   **Raster (PNG/JPG):** Pixels based. Loses quality when scaled up.

**How to convert a logo to SVG?** PopTheLogo provides these files automatically. Always ensure you have a **transparent background logo** for versatility.
