# Logo Design for Signage: Bold and Visible

Your signage is often the first physical interaction customers have with your brand. Your logo needs to be bold and visible from a distance. Here’s how to approach **logo design for signage**.

## 1. Design for Distance
Your logo needs to be recognizable from across the street.

## 2. Use Bold Typography
Thick, clear fonts are easier to read from a distance.

## 3. Use High-Contrast Colors
Bright, contrasting colors stand out in any environment.

## 4. Keep It Simple
Avoid complex details that can be lost on large signs.

## 5. Consider Illumination
Your logo might be backlit, front-lit, or non-illuminated.
