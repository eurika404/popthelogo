## Tips for Choosing a Scalable Logo Design

When using a **cheap logo design** tool, keep these tips in mind to ensure your logo grows with you:

1.  **Keep it Simple:** Avoid overly complex graphics. A simple logo is easier to remember and scales better across different mediums.
2.  **Focus on Typography:** A strong wordmark can be just as effective as a symbol. Choose fonts that reflect your brand personality.
3.  **Check for Vector Files:** Ensure the tool provides SVG files. This is crucial for printing on large surfaces without losing quality.
