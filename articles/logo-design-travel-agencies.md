# Logo Design for Travel Agencies: Inspire Adventure

Your logo should evoke a sense of adventure, relaxation, and discovery. Here’s how to approach **logo design for travel agencies**.

## Key Elements of Travel Logos

*   **Imagery:** Globes, airplanes, compasses, mountains, waves, and sunsets.
*   **Typography:** Script fonts for elegance, or clean sans-serifs for a modern look.
*   **Colors:** Blue (sky/sea), Orange (sun/adventure), Green (nature), White (snow/peace).

## Logo Ideas for Travel Agencies

*   **Icon + Wordmark:** A symbol of travel with your agency’s name.
*   **Lettermark:** Use your initials in a stylized way.
*   **Abstract:** A shape that suggests movement or direction.
