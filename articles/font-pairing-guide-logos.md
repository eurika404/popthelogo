# Font Pairing Guide for Logos: The Perfect Combination

Choosing the right fonts for your logo is crucial. The right pairing can make your logo look professional and memorable. Here’s a guide to **font pairing for logos**.

## 1. Contrast is Key
Pair a serif font with a sans-serif font for a classic look.

## 2. Match the Mood
Choose fonts that reflect your brand’s personality. Script fonts for elegance, bold sans-serifs for strength.

## 3. Keep It Simple
Stick to two fonts maximum to avoid clutter.

## 4. Prioritize Readability
Ensure your fonts are legible at all sizes, especially for **responsive logo design**.
