# Logo Design for Ebooks: Professional and Polished

Your ebook cover is the first thing potential readers see. Your logo adds a professional touch. Here’s how to approach **logo design for ebooks**.

## 1. Keep It Small
Your logo should be small enough to not overwhelm the cover.

## 2. Use a Simple Design
A simple logo is easier to recognize in a small size.

## 3. Use a Transparent Background
A PNG with a transparent background blends seamlessly with your cover.

## 4. Place It Strategically
Typically, logos are placed in the corner of an ebook cover.

## 5. Match the Book’s Aesthetic
Ensure your logo matches the style of your ebook.
