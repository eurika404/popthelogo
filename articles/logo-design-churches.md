# Logo Design for Churches: Welcoming and Spiritual

Your church logo needs to be welcoming and spiritual. It should represent your congregation’s values. Here’s how to approach **logo design for churches**.

## 1. Use Spiritual Imagery
Crosses, doves, and other religious symbols work well.

## 2. Use Welcoming Typography
Fonts that feel approachable and friendly.

## 3. Use Calming Colors
Blue, green, and gold can evoke feelings of peace.

## 4. Keep It Simple
A simple logo is easier to remember.

## 5. Create Variations
Have a full logo for your signage and a simplified icon for social media.
