# Logo Design for Dental Clinics: Clean and Trustworthy

Your dental clinic logo needs to be clean and trustworthy. It should make patients feel comfortable. Here’s how to approach **logo design for dental clinics**.

## 1. Use Clean Imagery
Teeth, smiles, and clean lines work well.

## 2. Use Professional Typography
Fonts that feel modern and trustworthy.

## 3. Use Calming Colors
Blue, white, and light green can evoke feelings of cleanliness.

## 4. Keep It Simple
A simple logo is easier to remember.

## 5. Create Variations
Have a full logo for your signage and a simplified icon for social media.
